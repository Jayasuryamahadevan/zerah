GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
you might be wondering.

Every factory runs differently. That's exactly why we start by understanding yours.

Will this work with our existing ERP?+

That's our starting point. We assess your ERP's available APIs, import formats, or other approved integration options and design around them. The exact method depends on your system and access.

What if invoice details don't match our PO?+

Exceptions shouldn't be hidden. Workflows can flag mismatched quantities, prices, tax details, or missing references so an authorised team member can review them before posting.

Do we have to change the way our team works?+

Usually, the goal is to reduce repetitive steps rather than replace familiar processes. We first map how your team works today and agree on where automation makes sense.

How do we start with a pilot?+

Choose one high-friction workflow, define what success looks like, and test with representative documents and exception cases. Scope, timeline, and integration requirements are agreed before implementation.

What about document access and data security?+

We discuss access controls, data handling, retention, and approval boundaries when scoping a project. Sensitive business documents should only be shared through an agreed, appropriate channel.
