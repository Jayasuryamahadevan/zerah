05 / QUESTIONS

The details
matter.

Every ERP, team and factory is different. Here's how we approach a few of the questions that come up first.

Can this work with our current ERP?+

We start by understanding the ERP you use and its supported APIs, import formats or approved integration methods. Integration is scoped for your specific system; no ERP replacement is assumed.

What happens when an invoice doesn't match a PO?+

The workflow can flag quantity, price, tax or reference mismatches. Your authorised team reviews exceptions before an entry is posted.

Will our staff have to learn another system?+

Our aim is to remove repeated steps from familiar processes, not add unnecessary screens. We agree on the team experience while mapping the workflow.

How do we start?+

Choose one time-consuming process, agree on success criteria, and test with representative documents. We'll discuss access, scope and responsibilities before implementation.

How will business documents be handled?+

We define data access, retention, integration permissions and human approvals during project scoping. Please don't send confidential files through the website enquiry form.
